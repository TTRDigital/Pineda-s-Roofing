Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a typical roof installation take?

The duration of a roof installation depends on various factors, such as the size of your roof, materials chosen, and weather conditions. However, on average, a standard roof installation can be completed within a few days to a week. Give us a call today for your free consultation and estimate!

What are the signs that my roof needs repair or replacement?

Look out for signs like missing or cracked shingles, water leaks, mold growth, granule loss in shingles, or sagging areas on the roof. Additionally, if your roof is more than 20 years old, it might be time to consider a replacement.

How much does roof repair typically cost?

The cost of roof repair can vary depending on the specific requirements of each project. Factors such as the extent of the damage, the type of roofing material, and the size of your roof play a significant role in determining the overall cost. We understand the importance of transparency and providing accurate information to our customers. That's why we offer a free, no-obligation inspection for every project. Our experienced roofing professionals will assess the condition of your roof and provide you with a detailed estimate tailored to your needs. This way, you'll have a clear understanding of the scope of repairs and the associated costs before any work begins.
